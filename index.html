About Our Group

Campus Collective is a study and project group formed in our first semester. We came together to turn separate coursework into shared work that is better planned, better researched and better presented.

Our purpose is simple: help every member learn by doing real teamwork, with clear roles and honest feedback.

What we do

  • Meet every week to plan tasks and review progress.
  • Run peer study sessions before tests and deadlines.
  • Build and present group projects together.

Our objectives

  • Deliver every group assignment on time and to a high standard.
  • Share skills so no one is the only person who can do a task.
  • Practise communication and leadership for work after university.
